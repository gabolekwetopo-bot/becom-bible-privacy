Privacy Policy for Becom Bible

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Becom Bible app (package name: com.becombible.app).

1. Information We Collect

Becom Bible does not collect or store personal information on our servers.

  • Your Data Stays on Your Device: All your personal data (prayers, notes, bookmarks, highlights, reading progress, and settings) is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data.
  • Backups: If you use the backup feature, a JSON file is created and saved to your device's Downloads folder. This file is never uploaded to us.
  • AI Questions: When you use the BecomChat feature, your questions are sent to our secure proxy server (hosted on Cloudflare) which forwards them to an AI provider (Groq) to generate a response. We do not store your questions or conversations on our servers. The questions are processed transiently to provide the AI response and are not linked to your identity.

2. How We Use Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to anyone.

  • AI questions are used solely to generate the AI response you requested.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.
  • We do not track your usage analytics.

3. Data Security

All communication with our AI proxy server is encrypted using HTTPS (SSL/TLS). Your data on your device is protected by your device's security measures (screen lock, encryption).

4. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party service:

  • Groq (via Cloudflare Proxy): Used for AI chat responses. When you ask a question, it is sent to our proxy and then to Groq. Groq's privacy policy applies to their processing of the AI request. We do not share your identity with them.
  • Google Play Billing: Used for processing subscriptions (if you choose to subscribe). Google's privacy policy applies to the payment process.

5. Children's Privacy

Becom Bible does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. The app is intended for general audiences.

6. Your Rights

Since all your data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. You can delete your data at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page.
